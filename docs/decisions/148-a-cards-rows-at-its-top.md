# 148: A card's rows sit at its top

Status: Accepted (amends 140). Core tested; the look on Windows needs a run.

**Context.** Decision 140 made every card in a row as tall as the row's
tallest, and laid its rows out in that whole height: the slot layout centres
rows in their frame, so a short group's name and tasks floated in its middle.

**Decision.** A card's rows are laid out in the height they need (their own
height, as measured before the row's tallest was taken), from its top; the
room the row's tallest gives it stays at its bottom.
